# Code of conduct

Springroll should be a useful, curious, and safe place to collaborate.
Participants are expected to be respectful, assume good faith, give specific
and constructive feedback, and make room for people with different levels of
experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, or publication of another person's private information is not
acceptable. This applies in repository discussions, reviews, community spaces,
and private communication about the project.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or
ban someone whose behavior is harmful. Enforcement should be proportionate,
documented privately, and focused on protecting the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub
profile. Do not include a conduct complaint in a public issue. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as practical, subject to the safety of the people
involved.
