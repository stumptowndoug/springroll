About these settings
Default model
Used for chats and recipes unless you choose another model.
Research distiller
Condenses large web results before they reach the main model. Choose Off to skip this step.
Recipe turn limit
Maximum model turns in each recipe run, not chat. Off removes the turn cap.
Recipe cost budget
A USD target per recipe run. A model call may exceed it. Off removes the target.
