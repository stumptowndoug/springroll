# Mail rules

The owner's rules for sorting mail, in plain English. They come before anything else. Edit them
any time; the triage job also adds rules here when you move a message in Mail.

- Mail from people (not companies) that asks a question needs me.
- Bills and payments due within a week need me.
- Receipts, shipping updates and order confirmations are paper trail.
- Newsletters are feed unless the rules below say otherwise.
